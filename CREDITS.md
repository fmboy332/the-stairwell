- 3D level, movement, flashlight, the face, scares engine: Mochi

## Source material

"SCP-087" by Zaeyde, from the SCP Wiki, https://scpwiki.com/scp-087, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

- SCP Foundation wiki: https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/ (content licensed CC BY-SA 3.0)
- SCP-087 page: https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-087
  (author attribution verified from the page's own licence box on 11 Nov 2026)
- Licensing guide: https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide

This game is an original adaptation inspired by SCP-087, not a true story, and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by the SCP Wiki or the author.

**Not used:** the SCP-087-1 image on the wiki (087face.jpg, by CityToast) and the stairwell
photo (087stair.png). The face in this game is original and drawn procedurally in code
(`js/face.js`).
